Storyboard

Example: job B610197, Worthington lube oil pump (the spreadsheet's own values).

  1. Intro: job B610197 (0:01) - This is R.C. Blake's rotor root diameter program, using the sheet's own example: job B610197, the Worthington lube oil pump.
  2. Rotor basics (0:12) - Enter the rotor: 10 teeth, 1.5 diametral pitch, a 20 degree pressure angle and a 30 degree helix angle.
  3. OD and engaging centers (0:21) - Next the rotor outside diameter, 8.905, and the engaging center distance, 7.58.
  4. Shaper cutter (0:30) - Now the shaper cutter: 9 teeth, with an addendum factor of 1.1.
  5. Rotor tooth thickness (0:37) - Enter the rotor tooth thickness from the backlash program: 1.1834863 minimum and 1.187075 maximum.
  6. Root diameter and clearance (0:47) - Results: the root diameter is 6.201848 max and 6.195811 min, with root clearance of 0.02959 and 0.02658.
  7. Set backlash and Help (1:01) - The resulting set backlash is 0.0120001 and 0.0060000. Help explains every term.

This tool is provided in good faith for general information only. No warranty is made as to its accuracy or completeness. Use at your own risk and verify any results before relying on them.

Inputs and terms

NRNumber of teeth on rotor
NCNumber of teeth on cutter

PATTransverse pressure angle

ODROutside diameter of rotor
CEEngaging centers
CTTTR @ PDRECircular tooth thickness, transverse, rotor, at engaging pitch diameter (from the BL to CR program)
ACAddendum of cutter (factor; inches = AC / DPT)
RDRRoot diameter of rotor
SET BLSet backlash resulting from the tooth thickness
